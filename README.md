# Dead Men Still Win Seats

**Dead Men Still Win Seats** is a web-based, turn-based strategy game about managing a Senate re-election campaign for a candidate who, as far as the public knows, is definitely still alive.

The player takes the role of the late Senator Phil A. Buster’s campaign manager. Each turn represents one week of the campaign. The goal is to keep the Senator’s death concealed, maintain public support, survive campaign crises, reach Election Day, and win the race before the truth becomes impossible to hide.

## Current Status

This project is in early game jam development.

The current Game Design Document is under construction and will change as the project scope becomes clearer.

## Core Pitch

The Senator is dead, but that will not stop his re-election campaign.

You must manage appearances, excuses, staff loyalty, public confidence, party confidence, suspicious reporters, electoral events, and increasingly absurd campaign crises while pretending everything is normal.

## Genre

Turn-based strategy / political satire

## Platform

Web

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. Review the week’s crisis.
2. Choose actions and assign resources.
3. Stage appearances, excuses, or campaign events.
4. Resolve risks and consequences.
5. Receive polling, news, and campaign updates.
6. Advance one week.

## Planned Features

- Weekly turn structure
- Crisis/event system
- Random and scheduled campaign events
- Public support and suspicion tracking
- Staff loyalty and party confidence pressure
- Campaign actions such as rallies, podcasts, AI videos, town halls, and news review
- Election Day win/loss resolution
- Satirical political campaign narrative
